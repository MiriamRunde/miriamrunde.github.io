About

I am a political scientist and data scientist. I hold an M.Sc. in Data Science for Public Policy and a Master of Public Policy from the Hertie School in Berlin, and a B.A. in Political Science from the University of Göttingen.

Since September 2026 I am a doctoral researcher at Heidelberg University. Before that, I led the research and data analysis for Compass-W at INES Analytics, an index for equal and fair work.

Research interests

  • Comparative party research
  • Cleavage formation and the transformation of party systems
  • Computational social science and NLP for political text
  • Causal inference and spatial data analysis
  • Algorithmic accountability and AI interpretability in governance
